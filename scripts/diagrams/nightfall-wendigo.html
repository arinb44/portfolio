How the wendigo hunts

Chase

  • Runs at you along the navigation mesh, speed 6
  • Gives up while you are inside the shack
  • Within 7 units: attack
⇄

Attack

  • Swings after a one-second wind-up
  • 25 damage if you are still within 8 units
  • Then back to the chase

Stun

  • One hit in ten staggers it backwards
  • Then back to the chase

Second wind

  • The first time its health falls to 60, it screams for three seconds
  • Health back to 600, speed up to 8
Starts with 500 health. Every hit sprays blood and takes health off.
Death at zero. The death animation plays and the world script starts the ending.
